How it works (3D)

  • The tree is simulated once, year by year, as a 3D skeleton of nodes. Each node records its birth and death time.
  • Every year, active buds extend in 3D space, steered by random 3D jitter, phototropism (+Y up) and gravity droop (-Y down).
  • Lateral buds roll around their parent shoot axis by the golden angle (~137.5°) and tilt out by the branch angle, with reduced vigor (apical dominance).
  • A 3D voxel light model counts shading nodes above each shoot to slow, cull, and shed shaded lower branches so the crown lifts naturally.
  • Time is only a render parameter: Three.js rebuilds a single tapered-cylinder mesh using pipe-model radii and updates an instanced leaf mesh every frame.
