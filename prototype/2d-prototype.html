How it works

  • The tree is simulated once, year by year, as a skeleton of nodes. Each node records its birth time.
  • Every year, active buds extend by one internode, steered by random jitter, phototropism (up) and droop (down).
  • Buds spawn lateral buds with reduced vigor (apical dominance). Shoots of higher order are shorter and die sooner. A cheap light model (shade from live nodes above) slows and culls shaded buds, and shades out whole lower branches, which retract and fall so the crown lifts.
  • Time is just a render parameter: segments grow by age, and radii are recomputed bottom-up with the pipe model, so the trunk thickens as branches accumulate.
